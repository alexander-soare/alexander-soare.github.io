I'm all in on AI for Robotics. I'm currently working as an AI Research Engineer at [Cobot](https://www.co.bot/), where we envision a future where robots and humans work together side by side to move the world. Here, I work in an agile research team that's hands on with real hardware, while staying abreast of the latest and greatest in embodied AI.

My quest is to discover what it's going to take to solve robotics, and more broadly, intelligence. I focus on projects that can make an impact in our collective effort to find the answers, and those that pique my intellectual curiosity. I actively share insights from my projects with the community whenever possible. You may follow my progress on [X](https://x.com/asoare159), [Github](https://github.com/alexander-soare), and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexander-soare-996145a7).
